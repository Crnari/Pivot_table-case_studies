# Case 4 — Stock Market Data

**Dataset:** 29,440 rows · 510 symbols · 21 August to 20 November 2009
**Sheet:** `Stock Pivot`

## Q1. Using the calculated field Daily Spread (High - Low), on which date did Amazon see the largest price spread?

23 October 2009, at $9.03, with a high of $119.65 and a low of $110.62. The next largest is $7.19 on 26 October, so the date stands clear of the rest of the sample.

![Q1](img/04-stocks/q1.png)

## Q2. Sort dates oldest to newest and apply a color scale to Daily Spread, from white to blue.

A wide spread is not good or bad on its own, so the neutral scale avoids implying a judgement the metric does not carry.

![Q2](img/04-stocks/q2.png)

## Q3. Adding a second instance of Volume shown as Difference From the previous date, when did Google see its largest day-over-day gain in trading volume? And its largest drop?

The largest gain was on 15 October 2009, at +28,353. The largest drop was on 19 October 2009, at -56,256. Both fall in the same two-week stretch and the drop is about twice the size of the gain.

![Q3](img/04-stocks/q3.png)
